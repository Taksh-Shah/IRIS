## Question

Is BLE-002's iOS CoreBluetooth v1 design surface — connect-to-identify discovery, background advertising suspension/overflow-area, state restoration, ~512-B MTU segmentation, iOS-as-limited-relay — still accurate through iOS 17/18/19/26 (2024-2026)? What must the DESIGN stage bake in: background-mode deltas (iOS 26 UIBackgroundModes + Live Activity), connection ceilings, MTU negotiation semantics, CVE/state-restoration hardenings, and cross-platform (Android⇄iOS) discovery interop vectors?

## 1. Verdict: **CONDITIONAL — PROCEED**

The 2024-2026 CoreBluetooth reality does **not** relax the constraints BLE-002_DISCOVER.md/BLE.md already state. Background advertising remains hidden in the "overflow area" (iOS-iOS only, best-effort cadence); background scanning still requires a service-UUID filter with batched/delayed results and no `allowDuplicates`; `startAdvertising(_:)` still supports *only* local name + service UUIDs — no service data, ever. New in this window: (a) an iOS 18.0 background-advertising regression (fixed 18.1) — a transient bug, not a relaxation; (b) iOS 26 (Sept 2025) additions that are **not** general-purpose relaxations — new `bluetooth-peripheral` background-mode categories ("Common Bluetooth HIDs" / "Bluetooth LE Custom Devices") tied to AccessorySetupKit accessory pairing (IRIS does not qualify), and Live Activity extending *scanning* while the screen is on / Lock Screen visible (stops at screen sleep). No Android-`PendingIntent` equivalent exists on iOS; state restoration does not auto-resume scanning; the system relaunches apps only for *connection* events after *system* (not user) termination. Android still cannot see a backgrounded iOS node's advertisement, and iOS cannot advertise service data to Android — so **connect-to-identify, a stable advertised IRIS service UUID, foreground-always symmetric discovery, `supports_background_ios=false`, and the 23→512-B MTU segmenter (with negotiated-late 23-B fallback) remain the correct BLE-002 design.** Condition: adopt the iOS 26 "screen-on scan via Live Activity" framing in docs (not as an OS background relaxation), keep the segmenter cap at 512 B, and design for the iOS 16.x-era low-MTU (20-B) regression as degraded-but-functional.

## 2. RQ-1 — CoreBluetooth background scan/advertise: any relaxation in iOS 17/18/19/26?

**No general relaxation.** The Apple "Core Bluetooth Background Execution" model (L1) is unchanged: background advertising uses the overflow area; background scanning requires a service-UUID filter and lower priority. Two deltas (iOS 18.0 regression; iOS 26 accessory categories + Live Activity) are contextual, not relaxation.

## 3. RQ-2 — Can a backgrounded iOS remain discoverable? Is there an Android-PendingIntent equivalent?

**No** general escape hatch; the hidden overflow-advertisement limitation is current through iOS 26.

| # | Finding | Source (evidence) | Maturity |
|---|---------|-------------------|----------|
| 1 | Apple's overflow-area description (unchanged through iOS 26): background: `CBAdvertisementDataLocalNameKey` ignored; service UUIDs "placed in a special 'overflow' area; they can be discovered **only by an iOS device** that is explicitly scanning for them"; advertising frequency may decrease; advertisement is "best effort". | Apple "Core Bluetooth Background Execution for iOS Apps" (L1 doc) | L1 |
| 2 | Overflow area is a proprietary format — Apple manufacturer data `FF 4C 00 01` + a 128-bit hashed bitmask of service UUIDs; receiver-side hash must match *a* scanned-for UUID (payload is not raw service data); a custom scanner can parse it (iOS, or non-Apple with custom code). | Unknown Labs (davidgyoung) reverse-engineering writeups; Apple forum 759280 | L5 |
| 3 | iOS has **no** equivalent of Android's `PendingIntent`-filtered background scan. Scanning occurs only while the app is running; for *system*-terminated apps the system relaunches for supported events — "relaunch for connections, not for scans"; user force-quit disables relaunch entirely. | Apple Developer Forums threads 715758 (2025-03, eskimo) + 64074 (eskimo) | L4 |
| 4 | "Always-advertise" iOS utilities (e.g. defunct Beyond Connect-style monitors) rely on the overflow area or foreground-only advertising — no privileged escape hatch. Backgrounded iOS *cannot* transmit iBeacon; region monitoring is receive-only from the app's perspective. | Community (Stack Overflow 2016-2026); Apple Background Execution doc | L5 |
| 5 | iOS 26 Live Activity: continues a CoreBluetooth scan in background even without a service-UUID filter, but only while the screen is on / Lock Screen visible; all scanning stops when the screen sleeps. | Apple Developer Forums thread 815189 (2026-02) | L5 |

**App-level implications:**
1. All verified iOS Bluetooth CVEs are **OS-patchable via the iOS update channel only** — no in-app mitigation. App-layer defense-in-depth (AEAD + per-message auth, SEC-001/CRYPTO-001 posture) is the only in-app control; document an iOS security-patch floor in deployment docs (mirrors BLE-001 AC-14/BLK-0005).
2. State restoration does **not** auto-resume scanning/advertising: `willRestoreState` fires only for system (not user) termination; the app must re-`startScan`/re-`startAdvertising` in the handler. A restoration-ID mismatch or restart-while-scanning crash-loop is a known DoS pattern (not a CVE) — DESIGN must debounce/timeout restoration and never initialize BLE state outside a valid launch path.
3. iOS 26 does **not** change this for general apps (engineer-confirmed); the new accessory categories restore *accessory pairing state*, not app CoreBluetooth state (RQ-1 row 4).

## 7. Design Inputs for BLE-002 (iter ~134 DESIGN)

1. **Connect-to-identify is the permanent iOS discovery flow.** IRIS service-UUID-filtered scan (foreground) → connect → read identification/service characteristics. Use a **stable IRIS service UUID** (never a per-session dynamic UUID — a background scanner would need the ever-changing UUID in its filter). Keep DiscoveryBeacon ServiceData on Android; on iOS, discovery payload rides in characteristics, not advertisements.
2. **Advertisement budget is tiny:** 28-B initial + 10-B scan-response (local name only). 1×128-bit IRIS service UUID (18 B) + ≤10-B UTF-8 local name; no room for DiscoveryBeacon-style payload duplication. Do not rely on the overflow-area hash for Android interop.
3. **`supports_background_ios` stays `false`** (ble.rs:287; test :1075 pins it). Backgrounded iOS is discoverable only by iOS peers (overflow area, best-effort cadence); Android peers cannot see it; symmetric peer discovery requires both foregrounded (or iOS screen-on + Live Activity).
4. **Foreground-always posture + iOS 26 Live Activity framing.** Document "screen-on scan via Live Activity (iOS 26)" as the only background-ish scan path — it stops at screen sleep. Recommend foreground + screen-on for delivery-critical p2p; MCF/network+BLE remains the fallback.
5. **MTU/segmenter:** iOS negotiates ATT MTU automatically (no request API), honoring up to 517; `maximumWriteValueLength(for:)` returns negotiated payload (cap 512). Segmenter: cap chunk at **512 B**; keep negotiated-late 23-B fallback for non-negotiating peers; add a guard for the iOS 16.x-era 20-B MTU regression (degraded-but-functional, documented).
6. **State-restoration hardening (app-side):** assign restoration identifier at manager creation; re-`startScan`/re-`startAdvertising` in `willRestoreState` on every relaunch (system does not auto-resume); distinguish system-kill relaunch (allowed) from user force-quit (never relaunched — no hang-on-launch logic); debounce/timeout restoration to avoid relaunch-crash loops; never initialize BLE state outside a valid launch path.
7. **CVE posture:** OS-patch-only for the verified iOS Bluetooth CVEs (2023-2026 set above); app-layer defense-in-depth (AEAD + per-message auth) is the only in-app mitigation; exclude the misattributed IDs (CVE-2023-28412, CVE-2024-44270, CVE-2021-31714) from any threat model; document minimum iOS security-patch floor.

## 9. Gaps

- **G1**: The 1-2% background duty-cycle and 10-30-s batch-latency figures remain community estimates (L5) — no primary Apple figure; acceptable as design planning, not as acceptance metrics.
- **G2**: Exact concurrent-connection ceiling (~8 peripheral / ~7-8 central) is community-measured, not Apple-documented; DESIGN should bound by the documented-guidance reuse/close pattern regardless.
- **G3**: iOS 26 Live Activity scan semantics (screen-on boundary, behavior behind another app vs Lock Screen vs sleep) are forum-documented (L5) pending first-party release-note confirmation; re-verify at IOS-001.
- **G4**: Overflow-area format (FF 4C 00 01 hashed bitmask) is reverse-engineered (L5); do not build interop on it — treat as iOS-internal.
- **G5**: Physical-device validation of all findings (CoreBluetooth needs real iPhone; simulator unsupported) remains BLK-0005 device-gated.

## 10. Verdict

**CONDITIONAL — PROCEED.** No 2024-2026 SOTA evidence forces a pivot of BLE-002's v1 shape (connect-to-identify + stable service UUID + foreground-always + 512-B segmentation + `supports_background_ios=false`). The conditions (screen-on Live Activity framing, 512-B cap with 23-B fallback, iOS 16.x MTU guard, state-restoration hardening, CVE-set corrections) are DESIGN inputs, not blockers. RES-0019 R7 and BLE-002_DISCOVER.md iOS constraints are re-confirmed with 2024-2026 evidence.
